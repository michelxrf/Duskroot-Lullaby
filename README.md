<b>Youngroot Lulabby - Time to Bloom!</b>

A narrative multiplayer online co-op RPG where up to four children play as heroes and receive a mission from a fairy to save the Youngroot forest from infection.

At first glance, Youngroot feels warm and colorful—lush environments, soft lighting, and inviting landscapes. But the deeper you go, the more the world begins to change. Colors fade, shapes grow organic and distorted, and an unseen force starts to take hold.

This contrast between comfort and decay is at the heart of the experience. What seems safe may not be, and what feels familiar slowly becomes something else entirely.

Play solo or with up to 4 players online as you fight, explore, and uncover the truth behind the spreading infection.

Play at -> <url>https://michel-redmer.itch.io/duskroot-lullaby-time-to-bloom</url>

Controls
Keyboard & Mouse
WASD – Move
Mouse (Right Click) – Aim
Mouse (Left Click) – Attack
E – Interact

Controller
Left Stick – Move
Right Stick – Aim
RT (Right Trigger) – Attack
RB (Right Bumper) – Interact
